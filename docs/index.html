FAQ

Common questions.

Everything about how Clawake keeps your Mac awake and what it does not touch.

Does it keep a Claude Code session running with the lid closed?

Yes. Clawake keeps the Mac awake, so a long Claude Code run, an agent task, a build, or any terminal process keeps going after you close the lid. Pair it with remote access to check in from your phone or another computer.

Does it work without an external monitor or power adapter?

Yes. Clawake keeps your Mac awake with the lid closed, on battery or on power, with no external display attached. It is a universal app that runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Can I reach my Mac remotely while the lid is closed?

Yes. Because the Mac stays awake instead of sleeping, remote access, SSH, and background servers keep working while it sits closed at home or travels in your bag.

Does it work with a corporate screen-lock policy?

Yes. Use the Enterprise build, or turn off "Don't lock the screen" in the standard build. Then Clawake only keeps the system awake, so the screen still locks on your company's schedule while your session keeps running behind the lock.

Will my Mac overheat with the lid closed?

Clawake watches the temperature and pauses automatically if the Mac runs hot, then resumes once it cools. You can set the cutoff or turn the guard off, and it can also pause on low battery.

How do I uninstall it?

Drag Clawake to the Trash. If you enabled lid-closed keep-awake, it restores normal sleep and removes its helper as part of that, leaving nothing behind.
